Hello and welcome! I'm __Ji-Hoon Oh__, currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), working with [Prof. Jasper Kok](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=LibHGysAAAAJ&hl=en).  Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Scripps Institution of Oceanography, UC San Diego, working with [Prof. Shang-Ping Xie](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=vGEx6O0AAAAJ&hl=ko&oi=ao), and before that at Seoul National University (SNU). I hold both M.S. and Ph.D. from Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH), where I studied under [Prof. Jong-Seong Kug](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=PZF-9w0AAAAJ&hl=en).

My research interests are **large-scale climate dynamics**, **Earth system modeling**, **AMOC**, **ITCZ**, **climate irreversibility**, and **geoengineering (Solar Radiation Management)**.

For inquiries or collaborations, please contact me at jhoh0926@gmail.com.
